📢 Advertising & Ad Networks AdMob / Ad Manager

QuizKart displays advertisements to support our free service. We use Google Ad Manager and Google AdMob (operated by Google LLC) to serve ads within the app.

Types of ads shown in QuizKart:
• Banner Ads — displayed at the bottom of quiz and game screens
• Interstitial Ads — full-screen ads shown between game sessions or on lifeline use
• Rewarded Ads — optional ads you can watch voluntarily to earn in-app coins
• App Open Ads — displayed when you open or resume the app

What Data Ad Networks Collect

Google Ad Manager and Google AdMob may collect and use the following data to serve personalized or contextual advertisements:

  • Android Advertising ID (AAID) — a resettable, anonymous device identifier
  • IP address — used to infer approximate geographic location for ad targeting
  • Device information — device model, OS version, screen size
  • App usage data — which screens you visit, how long you use the app
  • Ad interaction data — whether you viewed, clicked, or closed an ad
Personalized vs Non-Personalized Ads: By default, Google may serve personalized ads based on your interests and previous interactions. You can opt out of personalized ads at any time through your device settings (see "Your Choices" below).

Rewarded Ads (Watch Ad to Earn)

QuizKart features a Watch Ad to Earn feature where you can voluntarily watch a rewarded video advertisement in exchange for in-app coins. Watching these ads is entirely optional. Completing a rewarded ad grants +5 coins to your wallet.

Your Choices Regarding Ads

  • Opt out of personalized ads (Android):
    Go to Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Reset your Advertising ID:
    Go to Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID
  • Google Ad Settings:
    Visit adssettings.google.com to manage your ad preferences

🪙 Coins, Rewards & Wallet

QuizKart features a virtual coin system. Coins are earned by correctly answering quiz questions, completing challenges, and watching rewarded video advertisements. Coins can be redeemed for vouchers or other rewards as offered within the app.

Virtual coins have no real monetary value unless explicitly redeemable for vouchers as stated within the app. Coin balances may be reset or adjusted in cases of detected fraud or violation of our Terms of Service. Rewarded ad coins (+5 per ad) are credited only upon full ad completion.
