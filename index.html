We do not receive or store your Google password. Authentication tokens are managed securely by Firebase. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

🪙 Coins, Rewards & Wallet

QuizKart features a virtual coin system. Coins are earned by correctly answering quiz questions and completing challenges. Coins can be redeemed for vouchers or other rewards as offered within the app.

Virtual coins have no real monetary value unless explicitly redeemable for vouchers as stated within the app. Coin balances may be reset or adjusted in cases of detected fraud or violation of our Terms of Service.
